# Internal Ops Runbook (ADMIN only)

## Database Failover Procedure
1. Confirm the primary is actually down via the /health endpoint, not just the alert.
2. Promote the standby replica using `pg_ctl promote`.
3. Update the connection string in the secrets manager; do not hardcode it anywhere.
4. Notify the on-call channel before, not after, customers notice.

## Credential Rotation
- Rotate the database service account password quarterly.
- Rotate the JWT signing secret only during a maintenance window, since every
  outstanding token becomes invalid the moment the secret changes.
- Never store a rotated secret in application.properties directly; use the
  environment-variable override.

## Incident Escalation
Sev1 pages the on-call engineer and the eng lead simultaneously. Sev2 pages
on-call only. Do not page finance or support for infra incidents.
